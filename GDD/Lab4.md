### [**GUNAROK REPOSITORY**](https://github.com/jennyAsas/GAMDES1_GUNAROK.git)

#### **1\. High Concept**

Gunarok is a 2D fighting game where the fiercest warriors in history, like Leonidas, Ragnar, Musashi and Lapu-Lapu, face each other in one-on-one duels, and the last fighter standing has to take on a god. What makes it interesting is that every warrior uses the same four simple moves (punch, kick, block and special), so anyone can pick it up fast, but each warrior's special move is based on their own legend, which makes every fighter feel different. The player gets to be a legendary warrior and prove that a human can beat the gods.

#### **2\. Formal Elements**

**Players \---\>** Gunarok is for two players, and each one controls one warrior in a one-on-one match. A single player can also play against the computer. Each player picks one of the 8 warriors before the fight starts.  
**Objectives** **\---\>** In each round, the goal is to bring the other warrior's health bar down to zero before the round timer ends. To win the whole match, a player has to win more rounds than the other player.  
**Rules \---\>** Every warrior has the same four moves: punch, kick, block and special. Blocking cuts the damage from an incoming hit but does not stop it completely. The special move hits harder than a punch or a kick, but it is slower and easier to dodge, so using it at the wrong time is risky. A warrior can't hit while blocking. If the timer runs out, the warrior with more health wins the round.  
**Procedures \---\>** The match starts at the character select screen. After both players pick a warrior, the round begins and each player moves, attacks and blocks in real time. When a round ends, the next one starts right away until someone wins the match. In the single-player mode, the winner then moves on to the next opponent, and the last fight is against a god boss.  
**Resources \---\>** The main resource is health, which each warrior loses when they get hit. The other resources are the round timer and the number of rounds each player has won. (If the special move uses a meter or a cooldown in the build, it goes here too.)  
**Conflict \---\>** The conflict is direct: both players are trying to beat each other in the same small arena. The challenge is in the timing and distance, like deciding when to attack, when to block, and when to risk a special move. In the single-player mode, the god bosses add extra challenge because they are bigger and stronger than the normal warriors.  
**Boundaries \---\>** The fight takes place in a flat 2D arena with a fixed side view, and the warriors can't walk past the left and right edges of the stage. The camera follows both fighters and never zooms out so far that they can't be seen, and the warriors can't move farther apart than the camera can show. The match is limited by the round timer and by the best-of-three round count.  
**Outcome \---\>** A round ends when someone's health reaches zero or when the timer runs out. The first player to win two rounds wins the match, and it is a clear win or loss with no draws. In the single-player mode, beating the god boss is the final win of the game.

#### **3\. Core Gameplay Loop**

The core loop in Gunarok is a fast cycle of input, action and feedback. The player presses a button, which is punch, kick, block or special, and the warrior does that move right away on the screen. If the attack connects, the player sees and hears the hit: the other warrior flinches and the health bar goes down. If it misses or gets blocked, the player sees that too, and it shows them the other player is now open to a counter. The player then looks at the distance and what the opponent is doing, and chooses the next move. Then the loop starts again.  
What makes this worth repeating is that every moment is a small decision. Should I punch now, wait for them to come close, block, or go for the special? The rounds are short, so a mistake costs you right away but you can try again quickly. Because all warriors share the same four moves, the player can get good at the controls once, and then enjoy learning how each of the 8 warriors' special moves changes the fight.
